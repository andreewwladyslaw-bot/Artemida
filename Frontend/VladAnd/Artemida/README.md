# Artemida

**Artemida** is a web-based homework management platform designed to simplify communication and collaboration between students, teachers, and educational administrators.

## About the Project

The main goal of Artemida is to create a centralized environment where students can manage their homework, teachers can organize assignments, and administrators can oversee the educational process.

The project aims to make homework management more accessible, organized, and efficient by bringing essential educational tools together in one platform.

## Our Mission

Our mission is to improve the way students and teachers interact with homework and academic responsibilities.

We believe that a well-organized digital environment can reduce confusion, improve communication, and help students stay focused on their education.

## Project Goals

- Simplify homework creation, distribution, and tracking.
- Improve communication between students and teachers.
- Provide a clear overview of assignments and deadlines.
- Help students organize their academic responsibilities.
- Build a reliable, accessible, and user-friendly educational platform.

## Project Status

Artemida is currently in its early development stage.

The project is being developed as a collaborative educational initiative, with a focus on learning, teamwork, and practical software development experience.
